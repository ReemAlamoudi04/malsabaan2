# Current-version editorial rewrite

31 October 2026. Applies to the interactive current design and malsabaan2; the previous alternative remains unchanged.

## Voice

Understated assurance: measured, personal and precise. Trust comes from discretion, careful consideration and candid advice. Avoid superlatives, guarantees of success, invented experience, awards, clients, licence numbers and urgency. Arabic is written as original professional Arabic; English is adapted for natural reading rather than translated word for word. Interface actions remain literal and explain the real storage/WhatsApp handoff.

## Preserved

The main Arabic/English headline and all existing vision text remain unchanged. The firm's identity, contact facts, working hours, service scope, licence placeholders, imagery and navigation behavior remain intact. No new credentials or services have been asserted.

## Rewritten

Hero supporting text; firm introduction; practice headings and all 17 service descriptions in both languages; audience introduction, business/personal headings and summaries; topic phrasing; values; professional-information explanation; consultation introduction, labels, consent, validation, handoff messages; contact wording; footer and introductory disclaimer. Functional controls retain familiar terminology where clarity requires it.

- Al Tamimi & Company, Saudi Arabia: https://www.tamimi.com/sa-en/ — clear practice terminology and client-oriented legal services.
- Turki Al Tamimi: https://tamimy.com.sa/ — Arabic service vocabulary and the progression from documents to legal assessment.

The new text is original. Other firms' rankings, credentials, scale, results and promises were not transferred.
